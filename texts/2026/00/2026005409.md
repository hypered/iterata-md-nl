**Artikel 1.** Deze wet regelt een aangelegenheid als bedoeld in artikel 74 van de Grondwet.

**Art. 2.** Het Aanvullend Protocol tussen het Koninkrijk België en de Europese Organisatie voor de veiligheid van de luchtvaart bij het Aanvullend Akkoord tussen de Europese Organisatie voor de veiligheid van de luchtvaart en het Koninkrijk België bij het Internationaal Verdrag tot samenwerking in het belang van de veiligheid van de luchtvaart "Eurocontrol" van 13 december 1960 inzake de voorrechten en immuniteiten van de organisatie, ondertekend te Brussel op 17 juli 2006, gedaan te Brussel op 23 juli 2025, zal volkomen gevolg hebben.

ADDITIONAL PROTOCOL

BETWEEN

THE KINGDOM OF BELGIUM

AND

THE EUROPEAN ORGANISATION FOR THE SAFETY OF AIR NAVIGATION

TO THE AGREEMENT

BETWEEN

THE EUROPEAN ORGANISATION FOR THE SAFETY OF AIR NAVIGATION

AND

THE KINGDOM OF BELGIUM

ON THE PRIVILEGES AND IMMUNITIES OF THE ORGANISATION

COMPLEMENTARY TO THE EUROCONTROL INTERNATIONAL CONVENTION RELATING TO CO-OPERATION FOR THE SAFETY OF AIR NAVIGATION OF 13 DECEMBER 1960, SIGNED IN BRUSSELS ON 17 JULY 2006

THE KINGDOM OF BELGIUM,

HEREINAFTER REFERRED TO AS 'BELGIUM',

AND

THE EUROPEAN ORGANISATION FOR THE SAFETY OF AIR NAVIGATION,

HEREINAFTER REFERRED TO AS 'EUROCONTROL',

HAVING REGARD to the EUROCONTROL International Convention relating to Co-operation for the Safety of Air Navigation of 13 December 1960, as amended by the Protocol of 6 July 1970, in turn amended by the Protocol of 21 November 1978 and amended by the Protocol of 12 February 1981 (hereinafter referred to as 'the Convention');

HAVING REGARD to the Protocol consolidating the EUROCONTROL International Convention relating to Co-operation for the Safety of Air Navigation of 13 December 1960, as variously amended, done in Brussels on 27 June 1997;

HAVING REGARD to the Agreement between the Kingdom of Belgium and the European Organisation for the Safety of Air Navigation on the Privileges and Immunities of the Organisation complementary to the EUROCONTROL International Convention relating to Co-operation for the Safety of Air Navigation of 13 December 1960, signed in Brussels on 17 July 2006 (hereinafter referred to as 'the Headquarters Agreement');

DESIROUS to conclude to this end an Additional Protocol to the Headquarters Agreement, necessary for the proper functioning of EUROCONTROL;

WHEREAS the official activities of EUROCONTROL include, notably, the determination, billing, collection from users and payment of air navigation charges to States, and whereas in that respect, EUROCONTROL must also be regarded as a public authority acting in the exercise of public powers;

HAVE AGREED AS FOLLOWS:

Article 1

1. In addition to the protection provided for in Article 26 of the Convention, EUROCONTROL shall, in the exercise of its official activities, enjoy immunity from execution in respect of its property and assets, including all its bank accounts, and any debts owed by EUROCONTROL to its Member States, notably, in connection with air navigation charges collected by EUROCONTROL on their behalf.

2. However, exception shall be made to paragraph 1:

   a) to the extent that EUROCONTROL has expressly waived this immunity in a particular case;

   b) in respect of a civil action brought against EUROCONTROL by third parties concerning persons and/or property, to the extent that such civil action is not directly related to the official activities of EUROCONTROL;

   c) in respect of a civil action brought against EUROCONTROL by third parties concerning damages resulting from an accident caused by a motor vehicle belonging to EUROCONTROL or used on its behalf, or in the case of a traffic offence committed with the aforementioned vehicle;

   d) in respect of a counterclaim directly relating to a principal claim initiated by EUROCONTROL;

   e) for the enforcement of an arbitration award rendered against EUROCONTROL pursuant to Article 9 of the Headquarters Agreement.

3. No third-party attachment shall be imposed on property, assets and debts of EUROCONTROL referred to in paragraph 1. A third-party attachment may be imposed upon salaries and emoluments owed by EUROCONTROL to a staff member.

Article 2

For the purposes of this Protocol, the term 'official activities of EUROCONTROL' refers to the activities necessary for the fulfilment, by EUROCONTROL, of the purposes and statutory missions assigned to it under the Convention.

Article 3

The Protocol shall enter into force on the thirtieth day after the date on which the Party which was the last to complete the procedures has notified the other Party that the necessary internal procedures for the entry into force of this Protocol have been completed.

IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the representatives of the Kingdom of Belgium and of EUROCONTROL have signed this Protocol.

DONE in Brussels, on 23 July 2025, in two originals, in the English, Dutch and French languages, the three texts being equally authentic.
